Questions for Founders

The company

  1. Which merchant segments (by GMV/vertical) are your primary focus on Shopify today, and what percent of your base is Shopify-only versus other platforms?

Problem and product

  1. What specific merchant pain are you solving first (cart conversion, AOV, LTV), and what is the typical baseline metric and post-implementation lift by cohort?
  2. How does your AI generate and serve recommendations (inputs, model types, refresh cadence), and what data volumes are required for strong performance on smaller stores?
  3. What workflows replace existing tools or manual processes, and how long is time-to-value from install to measurable uplift?

Market and timing

  1. What is your bottom-up SAM on Shopify today and across other commerce platforms over the next 24 months, and what changed in the last two years to make adoption easier or ROI higher?

Traction

  1. Current ARR, YoY growth, net dollar retention, gross revenue retention, and number of paying customers?
  2. Share churn reasons and 12-month retention by merchant segment; what interventions improve survival for smaller merchants?

Business model and path to profit

  1. Pricing model (subscription, usage, revenue share, or hybrid) and average ACV by segment; what discounts or partner rev-share apply?
  2. Current gross margin, blended CAC payback, and sales efficiency (Magic Number); what is the model to reach cash flow breakeven and the cash required to get there?

The founders

  1. Founding team bios, prior exits or domain wins, and the origin story for Rebuy; which strengths map to product, go-to-market, and AI?

Competition

  1. Who do you most often see in head-to-head deals, and what is your win rate by competitor?
  2. What are the top switching frictions that keep customers from moving between you and alternatives, and how durable are they?

The round

  1. What are the details of the current or next round (target size, valuation range, structure), and how would you allocate proceeds across product, data/AI, and go-to-market?

Why Sidekick

  1. Where could an investor contribute directly (Shopify-plus brands, SI/agency channels, retail media, data partnerships), and what are your top 3 asks from a lead?
